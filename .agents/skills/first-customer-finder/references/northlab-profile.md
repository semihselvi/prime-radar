# NorthLab sales profile

## Business and offer

NorthLab (northlab.pro) is a software and technology services business. The currently approved offer scope is:
- Custom software and web application development
- Enterprise platforms and business systems
- CRM / ERP implementation or development
- System and API integrations
- Workflow and business-process automation
- Software maintenance and technical support
- IT consulting

Do not claim a capability, certification, customer result, case study, integration, or delivery capacity unless it is evidenced on northlab.pro or supplied by Semih.

## Target markets

**Primary acquisition markets: Japan and Egypt.**

Do not spend prospecting effort on Cyprus or Türkiye unless Semih explicitly changes this instruction. Do not assume Japan/Egypt are interchangeable: separate the country, language, legal context, sources, and outreach approach in each report.

## Ideal customer evidence

Primary ICP: a company or organization in Japan or Egypt with a public, attributable, current signal that it is seeking an external software-development partner, replacing or integrating business systems, automating a specific workflow, or commissioning a custom digital platform.

Relevant buyer functions may include owner/founder, CEO/COO, Head of IT, CTO, operations lead, procurement, or project owner. Mark the role as observed or inferred; never invent a named decision-maker.

Adjacent ICP: a company with a concrete public operational pain or recent business trigger that plausibly requires one of the approved NorthLab services. Keep this outside the high-intent shortlist unless the evidence supports an active project.

NorthLab's published service focus on northlab.pro is rent-a-car, transfer, real estate, tourism, and service businesses. Treat these as preferred verticals, not proof of demand: prioritize a company only when a public signal shows a specific need. Other verticals may be tested when evidence is stronger. Do not fill lists with generic businesses just because they have a weak website.

## Commercial constraints

- Prioritize paid, realistically deliverable project work and near-term buyer conversations.
- No new paid lead database, API subscription, scraping proxy, or infrastructure spend without explicit approval.
- Prefer public, intentionally shared business information and original sources.
- No private groups, login-wall bypass, guessed personal emails, phone enrichment, or mass scraping.
- Do not send messages, submit forms, or create CRM records automatically. Prepare a concise, evidence-based draft and a verified official/public contact route for manual review.
- A public signal is not proof of budget, buying authority, or an open project. Distinguish potential prospect, reachable contact, confirmed active requirement, proposal, and won/paid work.

## Required report

For every primary prospect include:
- Company and country
- Original source URL and publication date (separate from checked date)
- Exact public signal summarized accurately
- Which NorthLab service fits and why
- Evidence-based buyer role (observed/inferred)
- Verified official/public contact route, or clearly state none found
- One short prospect-facing opener and specific CTA in the appropriate language
- Main uncertainty or disqualifier

Do not fabricate a prospect to meet a requested count. If evidence is weak, report zero.
